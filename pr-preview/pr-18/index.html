THE COURT IS YOURS

Just you.
And the basket.

No clock, no opponent. Find your spot and let it fly.

01 / MOVE
WASD or arrow keys move you around the court, in the direction you see on screen.Drag the stick in the bottom left to move around the court, in the direction you see on screen.
02 / AIM
Point the mouse toward the rim. Or hold Q / E to turn your aim.You face the rim on your own, even as you move. Tap or drag on the court to aim somewhere else, or hold ↺ / ↻ to turn a little. Tap near the rim to aim back at it. The dotted line shows your direction.
03 / SHOOT
Hold Space, or the left mouse button on the court, to build power, then release. More power sends the ball farther. You can also hold the shoot button.Hold the Shoot button to build power, then release. More power sends the ball farther. In free shooting and beat the clock, run at the rim and press shoot to dunk, or press it standing close to the rim.

Try 10 free throws from the Mode menu. Shoot ten from the line, release inside the dark band on the meter, and see how many you make. Each new round moves you a step back.

Or pick Beat the clock: score as many points as you can in 60 seconds, shooting from anywhere. A basket is worth 2, or 3 from beyond the arc. The clock starts with your first shot, and a shot in the air at the buzzer still counts.

Or play HORSE: a ring marks a spot on the court. Walk into it and make the shot. Every miss earns a letter of H-O-R-S-E, and spelling it ends the game. The spots start at mid-range and move back every three makes.

Or take on the 3PT contest: five racks round the arc, five balls each, and two deep balls, all in 60 seconds. You move from rack to rack on your own, so just release inside the band. A basket is worth 1, the last ball on each rack (the blue money ball) 2, and the gold deep balls 3. Before your first shot, pick one rack to be all money balls with ← → or by clicking it by tapping it. The most you can score is 40.

The ball returns automatically. Press Esc to cancel a charge or pause. Free shooting stats last for this visit, except your longest basket, which is saved along with your best free throw round, your best against the clock, your best HORSE game and your best 3PT contest.

Plays offline too. Once the court has loaded, it works without an internet connection. To keep it close, install it: in Chrome or Edge, click Install in the address bar or the browser menu; in Safari on a Mac, choose File › Add to Dock.on iPhone or iPad, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; on Android, open the browser menu and tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
