THE COURT IS YOURS

Just you.
And the basket.

No clock, no opponent. Find your spot and let it fly.

01 / MOVE
WASD or arrow keys move you around the court, in the direction you see on screen.Drag the stick in the bottom left to move around the court, in the direction you see on screen.
02 / AIM
Point the mouse toward the rim. Or hold Q / E to turn your aim.You face the rim on your own, even as you move. Tap or drag on the court to aim somewhere else, or hold ↺ / ↻ to turn a little. Tap near the rim to aim back at it. The dotted line shows your direction.
03 / SHOOT
Hold Space, or the left mouse button on the court, to build power, then release. More power sends the ball farther. You can also hold the shoot button.Hold the Shoot button to build power, then release. More power sends the ball farther.

Try 10 free throws from the Mode menu. Shoot ten from the line, release inside the dark band on the meter, and see how many you make. Each new round moves you a step back.

Or pick Beat the clock: score as many points as you can in 60 seconds, shooting from anywhere. A basket is worth 2, or 3 from beyond the arc. The clock starts with your first shot, and a shot in the air at the buzzer still counts.

Or play HORSE: a ring marks a spot on the court. Walk into it and make the shot. Every miss earns a letter of H-O-R-S-E, and spelling it ends the game. The spots move back every three makes.

The ball returns automatically. Press Esc to cancel a charge or pause. Free shooting stats last for this visit, except your longest basket, which is saved along with your best free throw round, your best against the clock and your best HORSE game.
